Green's Windmill

built in 1807

A tower mill built shortly after 1807 by the baker George Green. It burned in 1947, was restored and reopened in 1986, and is now part of a science centre.

William Booth's birthplace

c. 1820

William Booth, who founded the Salvation Army, was born in Sneinton on 10 April 1829. At 13 he was apprenticed to a pawnbroker. His birthplace is now a museum.

St Stephen's Church

built in 1837

The present church was designed by Thomas Rickman and dates from 1837. It was extended between 1909 and 1912 to designs by George Frederick Bodley.
