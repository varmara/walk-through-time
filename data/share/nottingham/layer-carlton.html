Carentune

1086

Domesday Book, in 1086, calls it Carentune. For centuries it was a small township in the manor and parish of Gedling, home to farmers and framework knitters.

A railway suburb

1840s

The textile trade and the railways grew Carlton in the 19th century: its population climbed steadily from the 1840s, and by 1850 it had become a suburb with its own houses, churches and schools. The tram line from Nottingham reached it in 1914.

St Paul's, Carlton-in-the-Willows

1885

Henry Herbert, 4th Earl of Carnarvon, built the church of St Paul, which was consecrated in 1885. It is listed Grade II.
