Linby

Linby is a village north of Hucknall, with an old stone cross at each end, and the remains of Annesley Hall's south lodge gates nearby.

See all 2 on the map

Bottom Cross

14th century

Linby's Bottom Cross dates from the 14th century. It was altered in 1663 and restored in about 1869, and stands in an enclosure at a road junction, built over a stream. Linby's two crosses were once thought to mark an entrance to Sherwood Forest.

Annesley Hall gate pier

17th century

This stone and brick pier, of the late 17th century, is all that remains of the gates at the south lodge of Annesley Hall. It stands on a chamfered base and is topped with a ball finial.
