Old inns

Three pubs in the centre each claim to be the oldest in Nottingham. A television investigation concluded that the Salutation is the oldest building, but the Bell has been a pub the longest.

See all 3 on the map

Ye Olde Trip to Jerusalem

built in 1189

Claims to date from 1189, a date that rests on tradition rather than records. It is built against the castle rock, with rooms cut into the sandstone.

Ye Olde Salutation Inn

built about 1360

Another claimant to the title of the city's oldest pub. The core of the building dates from around 1360, and unusually large caves lie beneath it.

The Bell Inn

built in 1437

Claims to be the oldest pub in the city. Tree-ring dating of its timbers suggests it was built around 1420, and its cellars are cut into the sandstone.
