Clifton Hall

built about 1505

For 700 years the Clifton family owned the manor of Clifton, and Clifton Hall was their manor house.

Clifton Grove

The Nottingham poet Henry Kirke White, a butcher's son, published Clifton Grove, a Sketch in Verse, in 1803, aged 17. He died in 1806, aged 21.

St Mary's Church

built in 1190

A medieval church, listed Grade I. Lewis Nockalls Cottingham restored it in 1846, and much of the decoration is by George Frederick Bodley.

Wikipedia: St Mary's Church, Clifton

Clifton estate

Planned from 1950, the Clifton council estate was at one time the largest council estate in Europe. Since the 1980s most of its houses have passed into private ownership.

Wikipedia: Clifton, Nottingham

Clifton Bridge

built in 1972

Opened in 1958 to take traffic off Trent Bridge, it was then the longest pre-stressed concrete bridge in the country. A second bridge beside it, opened in 1972, made the crossing a dual carriageway.

Wikipedia: Clifton Bridge (Nottingham)
