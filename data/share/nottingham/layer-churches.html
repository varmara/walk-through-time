Churches

Medieval Nottingham had three parishes: St Mary's, St Peter's and St Nicholas'. Each still has its church, joined in the 1840s by a Catholic cathedral, and by chapels of the Nonconformists, such as the Unitarians on High Pavement.

High Pavement Chapel

built in 1876

Built in 1876 to designs by Stuart Colman for a congregation that goes back to 1690 and became Unitarian in 1802. Worship ended in 1982; the chapel then housed a lace museum and is now a Pitcher & Piano.
