The castle burned, 1831

1674–79

When the House of Lords threw out the Second Reform Bill on 8 October 1831, rioting broke out in Nottingham and a crowd burned the castle, which belonged to the Duke of Newcastle, an opponent of reform. Three men were hanged; the Duke refused to restore the castle, and its burnt shell stood on the rock for years.

Old Market Square

One of the largest paved squares in the United Kingdom. The two towns were once divided by a wall across it, whose line a stainless steel channel now marks; Goose Fair was held here until 1928.

Broad Marsh

After large-scale slum clearance, the Broad Marsh shopping centre was officially opened on 25 March 1975. It closed in 2020 and was left partially demolished; caves found beneath it, dug into the soft sandstone, became part of the City of Caves.
