Caves

Nottingham stands on soft sandstone that can be dug with hand tools, and about a thousand man-made caves are known beneath it: cellars, workshops, dwellings and shelters.

Mortimer's Hole

A long passage to the bottom of the castle rock is known as Mortimer's Hole. In October 1330 Edward III's men came in by a secret tunnel, and Roger Mortimer was led out bound and gagged.

City of Caves

Caves under the old town, used over the years as a tannery, pub cellars and an air raid shelter. Two caves opening onto the cliff face held the only known underground tannery in Britain, set up in 1500.

Sneinton Hermitage

Cave dwellings dug into the sandstone ridge extended out into Sneinton, where they were known as the Hermitage. What little remains can still be seen along Sneinton Hermitage.
