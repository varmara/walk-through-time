The railway that began in a pub

In 1832 coal owners meeting at the Sun Inn, Eastwood, proposed extending the Mansfield and Pinxton Railway to Leicester. It became the Midland Counties Railway, whose line from Nottingham, at Carrington Street, to Derby opened in 1839.

D. H. Lawrence's birthplace

1885

D. H. Lawrence was born in 1885 at 8a Victoria Street, a brick house of about 1875 and one of the four houses his family lived in in Eastwood. It became a museum in 1976, and is listed Grade II.

Wikipedia: D. H. Lawrence Birthplace Museum

The Lawrences on Garden Road

c. 1880

28 Garden Road, a brick miner's cottage of about 1880 at the end of a row, was another of the Lawrences' homes. It is listed Grade II.
