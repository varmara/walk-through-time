St Peter's, Tollerton

12th century

St Peter's dates from the end of the 12th century; work in 1909 gave it the shape it has today. It is listed Grade II.

Tollerton airfield

1941

Licensed in 1929 and opened in 1930 by Sir Sefton Brancker, it was one of Britain's first municipal aerodromes. In the Second World War it repaired Lancaster bombers; its 18 concrete pillboxes are all listed. The airfield closed on 6 June 2025, sold to the Vistry Group to build 4,000 houses.

Tollerton Hall

1948

In 1948 the Roman Catholic Diocese of Nottingham opened St Hugh's College at Tollerton Hall, a junior seminary for boys from 11. By 1969 it was taking boys who were not training for the priesthood, and it closed in 1986. The hall was then a company's headquarters until 2017, when it was bought as a private home again. It is listed Grade II.
