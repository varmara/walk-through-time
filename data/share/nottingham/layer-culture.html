Bromley House

built in 1752

A Georgian town house built in 1752. The Nottingham Subscription Library, founded in 1816, moved in during 1821.

Nottingham Castle

1674–79

The medieval castle was demolished after the Civil War and a ducal mansion built on the rock. Rioters burnt the mansion in 1831; it reopened as a museum in 1878.

Broadway

The building began in 1839 as a grand Wesleyan chapel on Broad Street. Broadway opened here in 1990, and one of its screens is the only cinema screen designed by Sir Paul Smith.

Rock City

The building opened in 1876 as the Alexandra Skating Rink. Rock City opened in December 1980, with the Undertones playing the first gig, and holds 2,000 people.

Malt Cross

built in 1877

Built in 1877 to designs credited to Edwin Hill, it is one of the few surviving Victorian music halls, under a high arched glass roof on laminated wooden arches.
