St Peter's, Radford

1812

William Peveril gave Radford's church of St Peter to Lenton Priory. The church was rebuilt in 1812 at a cost of £2,000, and is listed Grade II.

Raleigh Street

1885

In 1885 a small bicycle workshop opened in Raleigh Street and gave the company its name; Frank Bowden took it over in 1888.

Player's bonded warehouses

John Player built his Castle Tobacco Factories in Radford, and the firm's three bonded warehouses, seven to nine storeys high, were finished here in 1939. Tobacco making in Nottingham ended in 2016; the warehouses were demolished, the last in 2022.

Radford grows

1891

Factories, offices and warehouses filled Radford: Raleigh, once the world's largest bicycle maker, and Player's cigarettes among them. By 1891 the parish had 38,718 people, and on 1 April 1897 it was joined to Nottingham.

Wikipedia: Radford, Nottingham

Saturday Night and Sunday Morning

1958

Alan Sillitoe's 1958 novel became a 1960 film directed by Karel Reisz, with Albert Finney as Arthur Seaton, who works at the Raleigh factory. Much of the exterior location filming took place in Nottingham, and shooting at a Nottingham factory ran two days over schedule and £3,500 over budget.

Wikipedia: Saturday Night and Sunday Morning (film)
