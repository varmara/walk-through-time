# Collections of old pictures

Collections whose pictures are not openly licensed, so the map links to them and shows none: a building or pin
gets the link when it is in one of the areas (conservation areas), the layers (story layer ids), has one of
the names (part of its name), or is about one of the people, unless it is in one of the layers listed under Not layers
(a district beside an area, whose pins and buildings fall inside the area's outline: Hockley is not the Lace Market).
