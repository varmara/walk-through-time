# Historic pub interiors

Pubs whose interiors CAMRA (the Campaign for Real Ale) lists as historically important, in its National
Inventory of Historic Pub Interiors and its regional inventories. Only the facts are kept here (the pub, its
street and postcode, the rating) with a link to CAMRA, whose descriptions are not copied. A pub is matched to
its building on the map by name, and by postcode or street where OpenStreetMap has them; pubs outside the map
are left out. Taken from Nottingham CAMRA's list of heritage interiors (link below), November 2026.

Rating: national (of national importance), regional (of regional importance), some (of some regional importance).
