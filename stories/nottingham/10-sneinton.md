# Sneinton
id: sneinton

The hill east of the city: a windmill, cave dwellings, a church by Thomas Rickman and the old wholesale
market.

## Green's Windmill
- source: en:Green's Mill, Sneinton
- year: c. 1807
- check: 1807 | George Green | 1947 | 1986

A tower mill built shortly after 1807 by the baker George Green. It burned in 1947, was restored and reopened
in 1986, and is now part of a science centre.

## St Stephen's Church
- source: en:St Stephen's Church, Sneinton
- year: 1837
- check: Thomas Rickman | 1837 | Bodley
- architect: en:Thomas Rickman | en:George Frederick Bodley

The present church was designed by Thomas Rickman and dates from 1837. It was extended between 1909 and 1912
to designs by George Frederick Bodley.

## William Booth's birthplace
- source: en:William Booth
- at: osm:way/123120708
- check: 10 April 1829 | Sneinton | pawnbroker | now a museum

William Booth, who founded the Salvation Army, was born in Sneinton on 10 April 1829. At 13 he was apprenticed
to a pawnbroker. His birthplace is now a museum.
