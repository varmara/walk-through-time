# Theatres and culture

Theatres, concert halls, a library and galleries, from a Georgian subscription library to a 21st-century art
centre.

## Nottingham Castle
- source: en:Nottingham Castle
- see: The castle burned, 1831 | Mortimer's Hole | Colonel Hutchinson

The medieval castle was demolished after the Civil War and a ducal mansion built on the rock. Rioters burnt
the mansion in 1831; it reopened as a museum in 1878.

## Broadway
