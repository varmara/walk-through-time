# Old railways and canals: the years they opened and closed

The Old railways and canals layer draws each line from the year it opened: solid while it was in use, dashed once
it closed. Its years come first from this table, then from Wikidata (the line the map's `wikidata` tag names),
then from OpenStreetMap's own `start_date` and `end_date`. A row's name is the line's name in OpenStreetMap, as
its card shows it; every part of the line with that name takes these years. Opened: the year the line opened to
traffic (or the canal to boats). Closed: the year it closed to traffic for good (for a canal, the year it was
abandoned). Source: the page the years were checked on. Each row checked by hand.
