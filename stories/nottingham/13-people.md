## Bendigo
- source: en:William Thompson (boxer)
- place: en:William Thompson (boxer)
- at: nhle:1271444
- group: sport
- check: 11 October 1811 | heavyweight championship of England | St Mary's burial ground | crouching lion

William "Bendigo" Thompson, born in Nottingham on 11 October 1811, won the heavyweight championship of England
in 1839. He died in 1880 and was buried in St Mary's burial ground, under a tomb with a crouching lion. The
city of Bendigo in Australia is indirectly named after him.
