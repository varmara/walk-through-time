Ilkeston, on a hill above the Erewash, received a market charter from Henry III in 1252 and still holds its charter fair. The town grew with the coal and iron industries of the Erewash valley, especially the Stanton ironworks. It lost its railway stations in the 20th century and regained one in 2017.
