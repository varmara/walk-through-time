Nottingham became a city in 1897, as part of Queen Victoria's Diamond Jubilee.[^city] By then it made far more than lace, and three of its firms, Boots, Raleigh and Player's, became household names; by the end of the 20th century Raleigh and Player's had moved the bulk of their making elsewhere, while Boots thrived.[^three]

## Stone, clay and iron, 16th century–1860s
Before the town's famous firms, its ground was its first industry: what lay under it was dug, fired and smelted close to where it was found.

## Tobacco, 1877–2016
John Player bought a tobacco business in 1877. In 1901 it merged with twelve other firms into the Imperial Tobacco Company, and by the late 1950s Player's employed 11,000 people.[^player]
