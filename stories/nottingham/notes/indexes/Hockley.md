Hockley lies below the Lace Market, where Nottingham's cloth workers gave the medieval street Walker Gate its name. In the 18th century Arkwright built his first cotton mill here, Methodists opened a chapel, and in 1849 John Boot opened his shop on Goose Gate. More recently it has become known for its independent cinemas.
