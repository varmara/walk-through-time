Women's lives in Nottingham are less recorded than men's, but they appear throughout its history: as writers, businesswomen, teachers, doctors, nurses and librarians, and as campaigners for the vote.


## Votes for women
Nottingham had an active women's suffrage movement from the 1890s. Some women worked for change through peaceful campaigning; others, as suffragettes, took direct action and were imprisoned.

## Work and care
From Lucy Hutchinson in the 17th century to the doctors, nurses and engineers of the 20th, women in Nottingham wrote, ran businesses and schools, cared for the sick and wounded and led professional institutions, often as the first women to do so.
