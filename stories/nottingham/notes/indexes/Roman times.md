No Roman town stood where Nottingham is now. The traces of Roman times lie around it: an early military site, farms and villas, a temple above the Soar and a ford across the Trent.

## The army
Finds on the Broxtowe estate have been read as one of the Roman army's early forts, though not everyone agrees; at Calverton the army left the outlines of its overnight camps.

## Farms and villas
People went on farming the Trent valley under Roman rule. Some farms stayed simple; others grew into villas with mosaic floors.

## By the rivers
The Trent and the Soar mattered in Roman times too: a temple stood on a hill where they meet, and a paved ford crossed the Trent at Wilford.
