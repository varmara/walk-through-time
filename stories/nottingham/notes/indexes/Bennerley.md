In the Erewash valley, on the border of Nottinghamshire and Derbyshire, coal and iron were worked side by side in the 19th century. The ironworks at Bennerley and the wrought-iron viaduct built in 1876–77 belong to that industrial landscape, much of it undermined by coal workings.
