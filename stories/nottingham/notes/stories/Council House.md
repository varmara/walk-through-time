Designed by Thomas Cecil Howitt and opened in 1929, on the site of the old Exchange at the head of the Old Market
Square.

> [!fact]
> source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nottingham_Council_House
> check: Joseph Else | Agamemnon | Menelaus
>
> The sculptor Joseph Else named the two stone lions on its steps Agamemnon and Menelaus.

> [!fact]
> source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nottingham_Council_House
> check: Little John | 10 tonnes | 7 miles
>
> Little John, the hour bell in the dome, weighs over 10 tonnes, and its strike can be heard 7 miles away.
