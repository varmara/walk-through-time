The Council House, designed by [[Thomas Cecil Howitt]], opened in 1929 on the site of the [[The Exchange|old Exchange]]
at the head of the Old Market Square. The sculptor Joseph Else named the two stone lions on its steps Agamemnon and
Menelaus. Little John, the hour bell in its dome, weighs over 10 tonnes, and its strike can be heard 7 miles away.
