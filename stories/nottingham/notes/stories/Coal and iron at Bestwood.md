The Lancaster family sank Bestwood's coal mine in 1875. Beside it the Bestwood Coal and Iron Company opened an
ironworks in 1881 with two blast furnaces, added two more in 1890, and kept it working until 1928. The company built
the terraces of Park Road, St Albans Road and The Square for its workers, and its offices, with their clock tower, on
Park Road.
