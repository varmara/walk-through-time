Nottingham's first station, opened in 1839 by the Midland Counties Railway, stood across Carrington Street; a
through station replaced it on this site in 1848. The present building, of red brick and terracotta, was designed by
the local architect Albert Edward Lambert and opened on 16 January 1904. Its carriage entrances have Art Nouveau gates of wrought iron. A pair taken down in 1951, made in
Leicester in 1904, is now in the Science Museum Group's collection.[^gates]
