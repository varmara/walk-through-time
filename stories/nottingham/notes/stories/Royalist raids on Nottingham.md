The Royalists of Newark raided again and again. Sir Charles Lucas broke into the town in January 1644 and tried to
set it on fire, and in April 1645 a party from Newark took the fort at Trent Bridge. Each time Hutchinson won back
what was lost, and each demand that he surrender the castle he answered with fresh defiance.
