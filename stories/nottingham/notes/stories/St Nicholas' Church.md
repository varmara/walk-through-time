A church of St Nicholas stood here from the 11th or 12th century. In the Civil War, Royalists took its tower and
fired on the castle's garrison, so in 1643 the castle's governor, Colonel John Hutchinson, had it destroyed. For 28
years there was no church here; the brick church standing today was begun in 1671 and finished in 1678.
