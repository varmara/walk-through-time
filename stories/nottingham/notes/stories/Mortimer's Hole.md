In 1330 Roger Mortimer, 1st Earl of March, ruled England with Queen Isabella as regents for her young son,
Edward III. That October Sir William Montagu led Edward's men up a secret tunnel through the castle rock to a door
high in the castle, and they seized Mortimer; he was sent to the Tower of London and hanged a month later. The long
passage to the bottom of the rock is known as Mortimer's Hole.
