Iron-working at Stanton grew into a huge concern from the 1780s. By the mid-19th century the works made 20,000 tons
of iron castings a year, and 2.5 million by 1905. From 1985 the French Saint-Gobain group ran it, and the last
casting, in 2007, was an emotional day. The pin is approximate.
