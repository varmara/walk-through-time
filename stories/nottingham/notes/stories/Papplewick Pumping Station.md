In 1881–84 a pumping station was built here to raise drinking water for Nottingham from deep wells. It had two beam
engines by James Watt & Co. and an engine house by [[Marriott Ogle Tarbotton]], ornate with cast iron and stained
glass. It pumped until 1969 and opened as a museum in 1976.
