Nottingham Corporation Tramways built the depot in 1900 when it carried the Carrington tram line on up Mansfield Road to Winchester Street. Trams ran until
6 September 1936, when buses replaced them. Part of the building is now the Samuel Hall pub, named after the man who
laid out Sherwood.
