The first house in the park, built in 1809 opposite the castle gatehouse, was the vicarage of St Mary's. In 1827 the
architect Peter Frederick Robinson published a plan for building over the park, and houses began to go up along Park
Terrace in about 1829; within three years there were 40 to 50 of them.
