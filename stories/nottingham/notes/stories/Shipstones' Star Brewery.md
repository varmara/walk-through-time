James Shipstone founded the Star Brewery in New Basford in 1852. The red-brick building of 1900 had a red illuminated
star on its tower that could be seen for miles. Greenall's of Warrington bought the firm in 1978 and stopped brewing
in early 1991; the building now belongs to an auction house.
