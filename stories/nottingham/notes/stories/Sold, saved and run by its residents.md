The 8th Duke of Newcastle sold the Park in 1938 to the Nuffield Trust, which passed it on to Oxford University. The
estate became a conservation area in 1969, and talks that began in 1986 between the residents' association and the
university put the estate in the hands of its residents, who run it from the Estate Office.
