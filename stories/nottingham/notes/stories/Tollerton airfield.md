Licensed in 1929 and opened in 1930 by Sir Sefton Brancker, it was one of Britain's first municipal aerodromes. In
the Second World War it repaired Lancaster bombers; its 18 concrete pillboxes are all listed. The airfield closed on 6 June 2025, sold to the Vistry Group to build 4,000 houses.[^village]
