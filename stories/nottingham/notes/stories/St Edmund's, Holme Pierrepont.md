Henry Pierrepont, 1st Marquess of Dorchester, largely rebuilt the medieval church in 1666, and in 1878 [[Thomas Chambers Hine|T. C. Hine]] added
its chancel. Its monuments remember the Pierreponts, from Sir Henry, who died in 1499, onwards, and the satirical poet
John Oldham. It is listed Grade I.
