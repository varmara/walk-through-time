Pierrepont is Norman French for "stone bridge". In 1257 Sir Henry Pierrepont married Annora de Manvers, heir to
Holme, and the family stayed for almost seven centuries: Charles I made Sir Robert Pierrepont Earl of
Kingston-upon-Hull in 1628, and George I made Evelyn Pierrepont a duke in 1715. The estate was broken up and sold in
1940.
