Sir Robert Juckes Clifton, of the Clifton family across the river, was elected MP for Nottingham in 1861 and died of
typhoid in 1869, aged 42. The Wilford Toll Bridge he had planned opened the next year, and in 1883 William Philip
Smith's stone statue of him was put up at its northern end. It is listed Grade II.
