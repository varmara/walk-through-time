In 1778 George, James and John Robinson took a 56-year lease from Frederick Montagu to build cotton mills on the
Leen. With Castle Mill, Grange Farm, Middle Mill and Forge Mill, and the dam and Moor Pond that fed them, they employed
around 800 people, many of them apprentices brought from the St Marylebone workhouse in London. In 1785 James Robinson
bought a Boulton and Watt engine for the mill at Bestwood, said to be the first rotative steam engine in a cotton mill
anywhere. Spinning had stopped by 1828, and most of the buildings were taken down in the 1850s.
