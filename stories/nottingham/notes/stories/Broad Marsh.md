This was boggy ground on the edge of the medieval town, where the Franciscan friary of Greyfriars stood until it was
closed in 1539. After large-scale slum clearance, the Broad Marsh shopping centre was officially opened on 25 March
1975. It closed in 2020 and was left partially demolished; caves found beneath it became part of the City of Caves. In
2025 Homes England bought the site to build homes.
