Nicholas de Cantelupe founded this Carthusian priory in 1343. Its prior, Robert Lawrence, would not accept Henry VIII as
head of the Church and was executed at Tyburn in 1535; he was made a saint in 1970. The priory was surrendered on
18 July 1539, and substantial remains of its buildings survive.
