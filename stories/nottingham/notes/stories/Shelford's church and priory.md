Ralph Haunselyn founded an Augustinian priory at Shelford around 1160–80,[^founded] and by 1310–11 the church
belonged to it. After Henry VIII dissolved the monasteries, the
Stanhopes acquired the priory, in 1537, and with it the church. Lady Anne Stanhope's tomb, by Gabriel Royle, was put up
after her death in 1588, and the family vault is dated 1677. In 1877–78 Lord Carnarvon paid for Ewan Christian to
restore the church.
