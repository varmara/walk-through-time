The Luddite movement began in Arnold on 11 March 1811. Across Nottinghamshire the frame-breakers went after the
"wide" knitting frames used to make cheap goods. That first evening, framework knitters gathered in Nottingham's
Market Place to hear speeches, marched up Mansfield Road to Arnold, and broke 63 stocking frames at the shops of the
hosiers they most hated.[^arnold]
