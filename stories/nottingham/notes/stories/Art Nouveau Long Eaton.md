The town's new wealth shows in its Art Nouveau buildings: York Chambers of 1901 and the Midland Counties District
Bank of 1903, at 38 and 40 Market Place, and the Carnegie Library on Tamworth Road of 1906, by the same architects,
Gorman and Ross. York Chambers and the library are listed Grade II.
