Ruddington's station on the Great Central Railway opened on 15 March 1899. From 1940 a depot was built here to fill
and store ammunition, and it closed in 1983. The station had closed in 1963, and the Nottingham Heritage Railway now
runs trains on the line. The rest of the depot's land is a business park and Rushcliffe Country Park.
