Built from 1940 to fill and store ammunition, the depot was closed in 1983. Its land is now a transport heritage
centre, a business park and Rushcliffe Country Park.

> source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruddington

> Ruddington's station on the Great Central Railway opened on 15 March 1899 and closed in 1963; the Nottingham
> Heritage Railway now runs trains on the line.
