The station opened in 1880 on the Midland Railway's direct line from Nottingham to Melton Mowbray, which let its
London expresses avoid Leicester. It closed to passengers in 1941, and its site is now housing. Beyond it the line
became the Old Dalby test track, where the Advanced Passenger Train was tried.
