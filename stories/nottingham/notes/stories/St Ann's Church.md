The church of St Ann was begun for the new district in the 1860s: Sydney Pierrepont, 3rd Earl Manvers, laid its
foundation stone on 23 September 1863, and it was consecrated on 26 September 1864, a daughter church of St Mary's.
The parish church today is St Ann with Emmanuel.
