John Boot opened a herbalist's shop on Goose Gate in 1849, and his son Jesse was born in the area, into poverty, the
next year.[^boots] After John died in 1860 his widow Mary kept the shop going as M & J Boot.[^boots] Jesse became a partner at
21, and in 1883 he built a larger store on the same site, the first of the Boots shops; Nottingham Civic Society put
up a plaque here in 2015.[^boots]
