After William Lee died in about 1614 the stocking frame spread slowly: in 1644 London had some 400 to 500 frames and
Nottinghamshire about 150. In 1728 Nottingham's magistrates refused the authority of the London Company of Framework
Knitters, and the town became a chief centre of the trade. England had some 14,000 frames by 1750, and in 1758
Jedediah Strutt's "Derby rib" let them knit ribbing. Short of yarn, the knitters looked for ways to spin cotton here.
