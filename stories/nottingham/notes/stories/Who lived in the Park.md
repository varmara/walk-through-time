The Park's villas housed the town's business and public figures: the publisher Richard Allen, who lived at Albert
Villa until his death in 1884; the MP Anthony Mundella, for whom Amelia House was built; and Captain Athelstan
Popkess, chief constable of Nottingham from 1930 to 1959, at 35 Newcastle Drive.
