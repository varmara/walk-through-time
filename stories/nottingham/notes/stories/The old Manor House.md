A house called the Manor House stood here, near the river at the south end of the village green, and is said to have
once been a home of the Clifton family. It appears on an 1885 map,[^appendix] and a photograph
of 30 July 1951 in the University of Nottingham's Manuscripts and Special Collections shows it still standing.[^photo] Manor House Close was built on its site;
next to it stands the only house left of the row once called Manor House and Main Road Cottages.
