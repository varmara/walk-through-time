Henry VIII sent new tapestries ahead of his visit in August 1511, but a survey of 1525 found the castle in "dekay
and ruyne", part of the Great Hall's roof fallen in. It was garrisoned again in 1536, against the Pilgrimage of Grace,
and in 1562 it was to host a meeting of Elizabeth I and Mary, Queen of Scots, which never took place. By 1600 it was
no longer a royal home.
