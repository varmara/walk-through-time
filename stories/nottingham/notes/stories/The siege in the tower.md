When Parliament's forces took Shelford House on 3 November 1645, Royalists led by a woman fired on them from the
church tower; [[John Hutchinson]] had straw set alight to smoke them out, and smoke stains in the tower stairwell may
date from then. Lady Anne Stanhope's monument and the font were damaged; the font was replaced in 1662.
