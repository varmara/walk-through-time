Sir Francis Willoughby built Wollaton Hall between 1580 and 1588, probably to designs by the Elizabethan architect
[[Robert Smythson]]. After a fire in 1642 the house stood empty for some forty years; Wyatville remodelled it from 1801. The
11th Baron Middleton sold it to Nottingham Corporation for £200,000, and since it opened to the public in 1926 it has
held the city's natural history museum.
