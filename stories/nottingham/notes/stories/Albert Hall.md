The first Albert Hall, a temperance hall by [[Watson Fothergill]], opened in 1876 and from 1902 was a Wesleyan
Methodist mission; it burnt down on 22 April 1906. Albert Edward Lambert designed the present hall, dedicated in
1909, and it stayed a Methodist mission until 1982. It is now a conference and concert venue.
