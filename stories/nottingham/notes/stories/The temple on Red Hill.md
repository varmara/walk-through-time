A Roman temple stood on Red Hill, above the meeting of the Soar and the Trent, perhaps with a small town beside it, and
at least three curse tablets have been found at the site.[^pl]

Remains of a Roman villa and other buildings, and Roman pottery, have also been found in Ratcliffe.
