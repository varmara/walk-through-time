St Helen's was given its clerestory towards the end of the 15th century. Its monuments include Robert Tevery, who
died in 1571, and Gervase Tevery, who died in 1639. A classical porch was added in 1785, the church was restored in
1875–77, and a memorial chapel came in 1923. It is listed Grade II*.
