Workmen laying the sewers and roads of the new Broxtowe council estate found Roman coins and pottery.

That was in 1937. Later finds included a mount from the scabbard of a Roman sword, and in 1964 a ditch gave up pottery
and pieces of a lava quern of the 1st century AD.[^rb] The site has been taken for an early Roman fort of about AD 50
to 75.[^rb] Another view is that it was a British hut settlement, not a fort. The pin is approximate.
