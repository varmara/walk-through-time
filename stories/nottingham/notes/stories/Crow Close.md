In 1907 the earthworks in Crow Close, a field at the east end of Bingham, became the first site in England to be
described as a deserted medieval village. A later survey found too few remains for a village and suggests instead the
house and farm of the Porter family, freeholders here from the 15th century, whose house was lived in until 1754.[^bhta]
