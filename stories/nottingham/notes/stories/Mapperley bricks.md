The clay of the high ground fed some of the area's biggest brickworks. In the 1860s Edward Gripper mechanised them,
with Hoffmann kilns, and brickmaking went on until 1969.[^walk] Mapperley bricks are said to
have built St Pancras station, and so, people said, Nottingham once stood on Mapperley Plains. The pin is approximate.
