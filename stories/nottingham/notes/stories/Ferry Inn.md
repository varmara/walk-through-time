The inn was made from two houses and an outbuilding of the late 17th century, and is listed Grade II. Wilford stayed
a village until the later 19th century. With woods around it and riverside amenities such as the
Ferry Inn, it attracted many visitors from Nottingham. The Notts County goalkeeper [[Albert Iremonger]] was its landlord
until his death in 1958.
