The Park began as a wooded deer park on the west side of Nottingham Castle, hunted while the castle belonged to the
Crown. In 1663 William Cavendish, 1st Duke of Newcastle, bought the ruined castle together with its park, and the
land stayed with his successors for almost three centuries.
