Aerial photographs show traces of two Roman marching camps in a field north-east of the Oxton Road and Whinbush Lane
crossroads: a smaller one of four acres inside a larger one of about twenty-six acres. Nothing shows above ground, and
the site is a scheduled monument. Camps like these were dug by an army unit for an overnight stop.

Two very similar hoards of Roman coins were later found in the village, less than three hundred yards apart: in
June 1959, while foundations were dug for Manor Park Infants' School on Collyer Road, and in about April 1960, while a
house was built in Crookdole Lane. The first was buried in an earthenware pot.
