The medieval castle was demolished after the Civil War and a ducal mansion built on the rock. Rioters burnt the
mansion in 1831; it reopened as a museum in 1878.

> Rioters burnt the Duke of Newcastle's mansion here in 1831, over his opposition to the Reform Bill. It reopened in
> 1878 as the first municipal art gallery in the UK outside London.
