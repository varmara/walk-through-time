The first castle on the rock was a wooden motte-and-bailey, begun in 1068 on William the Conqueror's orders, and
rebuilt in stone under Henry II. For centuries it was a royal fortress and an occasional royal residence. After the
Civil War it was razed in 1651, and in the 1670s the 1st Duke of Newcastle began a mansion on the rock. Rioters
burnt the mansion in 1831; it reopened as a museum in 1878.
