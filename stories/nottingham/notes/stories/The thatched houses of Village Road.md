56 Village Road began in 1319 as a timber-framed farmhouse, later enlarged and cased in brick; it keeps its thatched
roof and much of its old timber inside, and is listed Grade II*. Its neighbour, 29 Village Road, is a timber-framed
house of about 1500, also thatched and later made into two cottages.
