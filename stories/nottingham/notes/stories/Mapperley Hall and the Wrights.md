In the 1790s Ichabod Wright built Mapperley Hall at the heart of his estate. His grandson, Colonel
Charles Ichabod Wright, began selling land for houses in 1873, but most of the estate was released only in 1903;
some 163 houses were approved in Mapperley Park between 1906 and 1914.
