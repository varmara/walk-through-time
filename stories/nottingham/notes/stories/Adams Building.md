A lace showroom and warehouse built for Thomas Adams to a design by Thomas Chambers Hine, opened in 1855.

Built from 1854 to designs by Thomas Chambers Hine for Thomas Adams, a Quaker lace manufacturer, with a chapel for
the workers in the basement. Inspectors thought it a model example of its kind; since 1999 it has been part of
Nottingham College.[^2]
