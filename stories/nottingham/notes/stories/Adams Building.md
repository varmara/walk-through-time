Built from 1854 to designs by [[Thomas Chambers Hine]] for [[Thomas Adams]], a Quaker lace manufacturer, and opened
in 1855 as his lace showroom and warehouse, with a chapel for the workers in the basement. Inspectors thought it a
model example of its kind; since 1999 it has been part of Nottingham College.
