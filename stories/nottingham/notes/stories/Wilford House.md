Wilford House, on Clifton Lane, is believed to have been built by Samuel Smith, an MP for 44 years, of the Nottingham
banking family whose bank is believed to have been the first outside London. The banker Abel Smith died at Wilford
on 12 July 1788. His wife's younger sister, Elizabeth Bird, was the mother of William Wilberforce, the slavery
abolitionist, who stayed at Wilford House for several months between 1786 and 1789. Thomas Forman, who founded the
Nottingham Post, later bought the house from the Smiths, and in 2021 it was given a multi-million pound
refurbishment.
