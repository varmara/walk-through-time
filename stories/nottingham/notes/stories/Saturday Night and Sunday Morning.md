[[Alan Sillitoe]]'s 1958 novel became a 1960 film directed by Karel Reisz, with Albert Finney as Arthur Seaton, who
works at the Raleigh factory. Much of the exterior location filming took place in Nottingham, and shooting at a
Nottingham factory ran two days over schedule and £3,500 over budget.
