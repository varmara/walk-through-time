In 1960 the Central Electricity Generating Board proposed a 2,000 MW power station at Holme Pierrepont, with coal by
rail and cooling water from the Trent. A public inquiry of 14 days turned it down, partly because it would have shut
off the sand and gravel beneath. The gravel was dug instead, and in 1971 the pits became the National Watersports
Centre.
