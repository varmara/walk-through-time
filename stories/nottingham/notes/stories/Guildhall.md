Built in 1887–88 to designs by Thomas Verity and George Henry Hunt, chosen in a competition judged by Alfred
Waterhouse, it replaced the old Guild Hall on Weekday Cross and served as the city's magistrates' court. The council
used it as offices until 2010; it has stood largely empty since.
