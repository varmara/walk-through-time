Soon after the First World War the shell factory's site became a depot and barracks for the Royal Army Ordnance Corps.
A film shot on 9 August 1942 shows Churchill and Matilda tanks
for Russia being loaded onto a train here.[^iwm] It was still a central ordnance depot after the Second World War. Its central vehicle kit store moved to Bicester
in 1958, but the site went on as a general stores depot and vehicle workshop.
