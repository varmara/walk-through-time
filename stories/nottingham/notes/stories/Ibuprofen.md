Ibuprofen was first made at Boots in Nottingham in December 1961, by [[Stewart Adams]]'s team. Their later laboratory,
on Pennyfoot Street, is now BioCity, and in 2013 a Royal Society of Chemistry blue plaque marked the discovery. Adams
and the chemist John Nicholson filed the patent in 1961; the drug was first sold in Britain in 1969, as Brufen, and in
1983 it became the first such painkiller apart from aspirin sold over the counter in the UK.[^ibu]
