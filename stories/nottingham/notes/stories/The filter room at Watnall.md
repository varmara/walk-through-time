In spring 1940 Watnall became the headquarters of No. 12 Group of Fighter Command, which covered the Midlands, Norfolk,
Lincolnshire and North Wales. In a bunker here, the filter room sifted reports of enemy aircraft before they went to
the operations room further up the road. It opened in 1943 and served as an early warning centre until 1961, and in
2008 it was sold to be made into a hotel.
