Digging before the A453 was widened, in 2012 and 2013, uncovered a farm on rising ground south of the old road between
Barton in Fabis and Clifton. A large enclosure was made here in the 1st century AD, around the time of the Roman
conquest. In the 3rd century a smaller enclosure replaced it, with a building for drying and processing grain, and the
farm declined in the 4th century. Each period left one burial.
