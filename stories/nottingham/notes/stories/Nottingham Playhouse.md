The Playhouse began in 1948 as a repertory theatre in a former cinema on Goldsmith Street. Its present building, a
modernist theatre designed by [[Peter Moro]], opened here in 1963. In 2001 Anish Kapoor's Sky Mirror was put up between
the theatre and Wellington Circus, and in 2004 the building was restored with a Heritage Lottery Fund grant.
