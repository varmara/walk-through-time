Calverton Colliery was officially opened on 24 September 1952, and the village grew from 1,304 people in 1951 to
5,658 in 1961. British Coal shut the mine on 19 November 1993; it worked again under RJB Mining until it closed for
good in April 1999. The pin is approximate.
