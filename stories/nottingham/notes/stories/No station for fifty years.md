Ilkeston once had three stations. The Town station closed to passengers in 1947, the North in 1964 and the Junction in
January 1967, leaving the town without a station for fifty years. After a long campaign a new Ilkeston station was
approved in 2013 and, delayed by flood works and great crested newts, opened on 2 April 2017.
