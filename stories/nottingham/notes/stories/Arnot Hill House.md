The mill owner John Hawksley built a large cotton mill here, working before the end of 1791 with a 60-horsepower
engine, and laid out Arnot Hill Park in about 1792 as a setting for his house. His son [[Thomas Hawksley]], born at
Arnot Hill House in 1807, became the engineer who gave Nottingham clean water. The house is listed Grade II.
