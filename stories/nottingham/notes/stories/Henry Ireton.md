Henry Ireton, eldest son of German Ireton of Attenborough, was baptised in St Mary's Church here on 3 November 1611.
He fought for Parliament at Edgehill in October 1642. On the night before Naseby, in June 1645, he surprised the
Royalist army; the next day he was wounded and captured, and Cromwell later freed him. He married Bridget,
Cromwell's eldest daughter, was one of the commissioners who signed the death warrant of [[Charles I]], and died
of disease outside Limerick on 26 November 1651.
