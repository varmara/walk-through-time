George Thomas Hine designed the Nottingham Borough Lunatic Asylum, opened in August 1880 with its wards
along one long corridor. It was extended in 1889, joined the NHS as Mapperley Hospital in 1948, and closed in
December 1994. The north end is now Duncan Macmillan House, the local NHS trust's headquarters, and the south end,
Nightingale House, is flats.
