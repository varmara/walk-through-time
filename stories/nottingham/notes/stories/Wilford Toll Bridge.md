For some 400 years a ferry crossed the Trent here, until a temporary wooden footbridge replaced it in 1864. On 16
June 1870 the toll bridge opened, and pedestrians paid a halfpenny to cross; it is still known locally as the Halfpenny
Bridge. It was closed to traffic in 1974, and in 1980 its centre span was replaced by a narrower footbridge. In
2014–15 it was widened again to carry the trams of Nottingham Express Transit, and it is now a tram, pedestrian and
cycle bridge.
