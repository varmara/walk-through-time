Albert Toft's stone statue of Queen Victoria was unveiled in the Market Square in 1905. In 1953 it was moved to the
Memorial Gardens on Victoria Embankment, and in 2022 it was cleaned and repaired, its sceptre mended and missing
fingers replaced.[^wire] It is listed Grade II.
