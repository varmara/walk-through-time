In 1898 Frank Matcham designed and built the Empire Palace of Varieties here. It closed for the last time in 1958 and
was demolished for road-widening in 1969. On 27 November 1982 the Royal Concert Hall, designed by the Renton Howard
Wood Levin Partnership, opened on the site, with Elton John as its first performer.
