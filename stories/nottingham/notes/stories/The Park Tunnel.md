The tunnel was meant to let carriages from the Park reach Derby Road through the sandstone. It was begun in about
1844 and finished by [[Thomas Chambers Hine|T. C. Hine]] in 1855–56, but at 1 in 12 it was too steep for horses and carriages, so it became a
footway.[^walk] It runs from Tunnel Road to Derby Road, with steps halfway up to Upper College Street.
