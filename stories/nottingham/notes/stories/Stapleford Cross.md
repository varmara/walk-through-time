The Anglo-Saxon cross in St Helen's churchyard dates from around AD 1000, though some put it as early as the 8th
century. Its shaft is carved with interlace and a winged figure with a spear, perhaps an archangel or St Luke. Its
head was lost in the 18th century; after several moves it came to the churchyard in 1928. Pevsner called it by far
the most important pre-Conquest monument in Nottinghamshire, and it is listed Grade I.
