Tollerton Hall dates from about 1675, or from 1792 by another account, and was much altered in the 18th and 19th
centuries, when Gothic Revival towers, turrets and battlements were added. After 1929 it was a sports club and
hotel. In the Second World War it was used for training before D-Day and then as a camp for German and Italian
prisoners. In 1948 the Roman Catholic Diocese of Nottingham opened St Hugh's College here, a junior seminary for boys
from 11; it took other boys from 1969 and closed in 1986. The hall was then a company's headquarters until 2017, when
it became a private home again. It is listed Grade II.
