In 1948 the Roman Catholic Diocese of Nottingham opened St Hugh's College at Tollerton Hall, a junior seminary for boys
from 11. By 1969 it was taking boys who were not training for the priesthood, and it closed in 1986. The hall was then
a company's headquarters until 2017, when it was bought as a private home again. It is listed Grade II.
