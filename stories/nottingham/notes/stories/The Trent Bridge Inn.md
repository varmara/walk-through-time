The inn stood by the bridge before any cricket was played here. The cricketer [[William Clarke]] married its
landlady, Mary Chapman, and in 1838 they had the field behind it cleared and fenced for the first match on what became
Trent Bridge. Clarke left for London in 1845, and his stepson John Chapman ran the pub and the ground. After the First
World War the Musters family, who owned both, sold them to the cricket club.
