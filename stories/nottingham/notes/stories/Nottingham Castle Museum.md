The burnt mansion stood a ruin until Thomas Chambers Hine restored it in 1875. The Prince of Wales opened it in 1878
as Nottingham Castle Museum, the first municipal art gallery in the United Kingdom outside London. The castle closed in
2018 for a redevelopment that cost £30 million, and reopened on 21 June 2021 under the charity Nottingham Castle
Trust. In November 2022 the trust said it was appointing liquidators, and the site went back to the council.
