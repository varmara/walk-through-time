Before the Old Market Square, Weekday Cross, by the Guild Hall, was the town's main market and its centre. A cross
went up here in about 1529–30, and by 1711 people called it "the Pillar". Butter, eggs, pigeons, wild fowl, fruit and
fish were sold on Wednesdays and Fridays. The Corporation had the cross pulled down and sold in 1804; Nottingham Civic
Society put up a new one in 1993. The Guild Hall's site is now Nottingham Contemporary's.
