The Hemlock Stone is a pillar of red Triassic sandstone, more than 200 million years old, on the hill above
Stapleford. A hard cap of barytes protected its top while water, ice and wind wore away the softer rock beneath.
Stories have grown round it: Beltane fires lit on it into the 19th century, witches and hemlock, a giant who threw
it, and the Devil, who flung it at Lenton Priory, where it fell short.
