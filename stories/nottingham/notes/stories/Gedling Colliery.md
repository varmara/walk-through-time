Gedling Colliery opened in 1899 and was the area's main employer for almost a century. In the 1960s it dug over a
million tonnes of coal a year with 1,400 men, about one in ten of them from the Caribbean, and was known as the "pit
of all nations"; 128 men died there over its life. It closed in 1991, and in 2015 its grounds opened as Gedling
Country Park.
