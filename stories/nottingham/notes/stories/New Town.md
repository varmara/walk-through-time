Until the Inclosure Act of 1845 the area was open Clay Fields. The Act let the town take 1,068 acres for housing, and
by 1880 the streets of terraced houses called New Town were complete. The terraces north of Victoria Park are listed,
as a once common kind of house now mostly lost to bombs and clearance.
