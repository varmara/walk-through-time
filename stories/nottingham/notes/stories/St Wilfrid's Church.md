Around 1361 Gervase de Wilford is thought to have founded a church here, and the building dates from the late 14th
century; it was restored in 1890–92 by Naylor and Sale. Inside are two memorials to the poet [[Henry Kirke White]], who drew much inspiration from Wilford and
Clifton.
