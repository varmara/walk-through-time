Basford made things for the kitchen and the bathroom. The grocer F. G. Garton started a pickle and sauce factory near
his home on Sandon Street and registered the name HP Sauce in 1895. Gerard Brothers opened a soap works in the 1890s;
Cussons took it over in 1955, and it closed in 2005.
