A medieval church stood here, though little of it remains; it keeps a screen from the late 14th century.
[[Thomas Chambers Hine]] restored it in 1872, and in 1896 a new nave and chancel were begun, their foundation stone laid
by Lady Byron of Thrumpton Hall.
