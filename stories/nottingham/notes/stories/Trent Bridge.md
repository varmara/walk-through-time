In 1156 a stone bridge of more than 20 arches was begun here. It was known as the Hethbeth bridge, and two of its
arches survive on the south bank. The present iron bridge nearby was designed by [[Marriott Ogle Tarbotton]], built
between 1868 and 1871, and widened to 80 feet in 1924–26.
