The iron bridge was designed by [[Marriott Ogle Tarbotton]] and built between 1868 and 1871. A medieval bridge of more
than 20 stone arches, begun in 1156, crossed the river nearby.

> [!fact]
> source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trent_Bridge_(bridge)
> check: Hethbeth | arches | 1924 | 80 ft
>
> The medieval bridge was known as the Hethbeth bridge, and two of its arches survive on the south bank. The iron
> bridge was widened to 80 feet in 1924–26.
