Built for the lace manufacturers John and William Lambert, who wanted a prestigious theatre for the town, to
designs by [[Charles J. Phipps]]; its portico of six Corinthian columns closes the view up Market Street. It opened
on 25 September 1865 with Sheridan's The School for Scandal, and in 1897 Frank Matcham remodelled its auditorium. In October 1952 it staged the world
premiere of Agatha Christie's The Mousetrap.
