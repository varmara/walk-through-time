From 1283 the borough of Nottingham sent two members to Parliament. In the mid-18th century the great landowners, the
Duke of Newcastle for the Whigs and Lord Middleton for the Tories, held sway, and the town usually returned one of each.
In 1885 the old two-member seat was abolished, and the city was divided into three seats of one member each:
Nottingham East, South and West.
