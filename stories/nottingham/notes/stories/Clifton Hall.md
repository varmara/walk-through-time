The Clifton family held the manor of Clifton from the late 13th century, and this was their manor house. Sir
Gervase Clifton largely rebuilt it between 1778 and 1797, partly to designs by John Carr of York. In 1958 the family
sold it, ending 700 years of ownership; it became a girls' grammar school, and is now two houses. The hall itself dates from the late 16th century and is listed Grade I. In its grounds
are a brick ice house of the mid-18th century, the vaulted remains of a curved garden stairway, a pair of Coade stone
lions of the early 19th century, and the late 18th-century stable block, now cottages.
