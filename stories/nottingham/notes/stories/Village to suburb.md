Wilford parish had 2,769 people in 1891 and about 4,500 by 1901. South Wilford became part of West Bridgford in
1935, and in 1952 Wilford became part of the City of Nottingham. The Silverdale estate was built on its farmland in
the late 1950s, and Compton Acres in 1986.
