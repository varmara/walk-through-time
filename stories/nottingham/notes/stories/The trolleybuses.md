Trolleybuses, buses drawing power from overhead wires, first ran on 10 April 1927 and took over the tram routes one by
one; the last tram ran in September 1936. A depot opened on Parliament Street in June 1929 and is still in use. By the
end of 1937 the city had 125 trolleybuses, the largest fleet in the country, and at most 157. The last ran on 30 June
1966.
