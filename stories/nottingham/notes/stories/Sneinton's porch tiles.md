Most of the terraced houses in more than thirty Sneinton streets were built between 1900 and 1914, and many have
glazed tiles in their front porches: over 250 designs have been recorded. Loughborough Avenue has water-lilies,
blackberries and Art Nouveau flowers like stained glass, Holborn Avenue stylised flowers, and Port Arthur Road
flowers in blues, yellows and oranges.[^best] Since the mid-1980s more than an eighth of them have gone: stripped
for sale, painted over or hidden behind new doors.[^lost] The pins are approximate.
