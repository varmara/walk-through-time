A ballad, Bateman's Tragedy, tells of a young man, Bateman, and a beautiful heiress of Clifton who pledge to marry;
she breaks her word for a richer widower, and on her wedding day he hangs himself at her door. His ghost haunts her,
and in the end carries her off. In the version told locally, the jilted lover drowns himself in the Trent and the
bride is dragged into the river at the chasm in the Grove; in another, a Clifton beauty is murdered and thrown down
the cliff.[^walk]
