A regiment raised in 1741 became, at the request of the people of Nottinghamshire, the 45th (1st Nottinghamshire)
Regiment of Foot in 1779. Against Napoleon it fought through the Peninsular War from 1808 to 1814, and at Talavera in
1809 won the nickname "Old Stubborns". Renamed the Sherwood Foresters in 1866, it was joined with the Derbyshire
regiment on 1 July 1881 as the Sherwood Foresters (Nottinghamshire and Derbyshire Regiment), whose museum is in the
castle.
