The county courthouse and gaol, rebuilt to a design by James Gandon. It is now the National Justice Museum.

> [!fact]
> source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Justice_Museum
> check: 1864 | Richard Thomas Parker | steps
>
> The last public execution here was in 1864, when Richard Thomas Parker was hanged on a scaffold above the front
> steps.

> [!fact]
> source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Justice_Museum
> check: 14th century | 1991
>
> Its courtrooms date back to the 14th century, and Nottingham's courts sat here until 1991.
