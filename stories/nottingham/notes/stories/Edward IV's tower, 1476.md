After Henry IV's queen, Joan of Navarre, lived here until 1437, the castle was little kept up, until the Wars of the
Roses made it a stronghold again. Edward IV proclaimed himself king in Nottingham, and in 1476 ordered a new tower and
royal apartments, which John Leland, writing in 1540, thought the finest part of the castle. On 20 August 1485
Richard III rode from Nottingham to Leicester, two days before he was killed at Bosworth.
