St Mary Magdalene's stands on the site of a Saxon church. Its tower was built in stages between the 12th and 14th
centuries, and its porch in 1320. Most of the Lords Byron lie in the family vault here, among them the poet
[[Lord Byron]], and his daughter Ada, Countess of Lovelace, rests there too. The church is listed Grade II*.
