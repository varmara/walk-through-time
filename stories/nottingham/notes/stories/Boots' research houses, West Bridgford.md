In 1920 Boots bought a site on Oakfields Road for pharmacological and bacteriological research, which was done here
until new laboratories were built in 1956; the site was sold in 1964.[^gateway] With its city-centre labs damaged in
the war, Boots put research into converted houses in West Bridgford.[^boots] From 1953 to 1959 Stewart Adams worked in
one of the front rooms of a house here, a rather primitive laboratory,[^odnb] while his technician used the larder as
an office.[^boots] On Rutland Road, in August 1958, his team began to study the phenoxy acids, the line of work that
led to ibuprofen.[^wiki] The day nursery on the corner of Oakfields Road and Rutland Road was also part of Boots.[^lb]
