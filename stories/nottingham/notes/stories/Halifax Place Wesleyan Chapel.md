Methodists met in Nottingham from about 1740, in rooms here and there, and in 1782 moved to Hockley Chapel on Goose
Gate. When the New Connexion split from the Wesleyans in 1797 and took that chapel, the Wesleyans bought land in
Halifax Place and opened a chapel for 1,600 on 2 December 1798. Its successor of 1847 had the highest ceiling of any
Wesleyan chapel in England; it closed in 1930 and was pulled down in 1966.
