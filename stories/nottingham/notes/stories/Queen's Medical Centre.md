Queen Elizabeth II opened the Queen's Medical Centre on 28 July 1977, and it took its first patient the next year. It
was the first hospital in the UK built to be a teaching hospital, with the University of Nottingham's medical and
nursing schools on the same site, and with more than 1,300 beds it was long the largest hospital in the country.
