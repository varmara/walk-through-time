[[Watson Fothergill]] designed this shop and warehouse for Furley & Co, provision merchants, in 1896–97; Lloyds
Bank has it now. Terracotta panels show the sugar trade, from cutting and boiling the cane to boats and a far
eastern market, and the Clinton Street side is faced with glazed Art Nouveau tiles.[^trail]
