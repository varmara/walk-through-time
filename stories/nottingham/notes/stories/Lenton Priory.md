William Peverel founded a priory of Cluniac monks at Lenton in about 1102–08, and richly endowed it. In February 1538
its last prior, Nicholas Heath, and many of his monks were thrown into prison, accused of high treason. The prior,
eight monks and four Lenton labourers were executed, in Nottingham and in front of the priory, and the priory was
dissolved that year. Little is left: the Priory Church of St Anthony is thought to keep part of its hospital chapel.
