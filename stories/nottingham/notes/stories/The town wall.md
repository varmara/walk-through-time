Nottingham's stone town wall was begun in 1260 and took 60 years to build.[^tl] A short length survives at the northern
end of Maid Marian Way, protected as a scheduled monument. Chapel Bar, the last of the town's medieval gates, was pulled
down in 1743 to widen the road.[^tl]
