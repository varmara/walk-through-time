[[Brian Clough]] managed Nottingham Forest from January 1975 to May 1993. Under him the club won the League in
1977–78 and the European Cup in 1979 and 1980. Les Johnson's statue of him, at the corner of King Street and Queen
Street, was unveiled by his widow Barbara on 6 November 2008.
