The Bogs, the wet meadow by the Leen, were common land. When the lord of the manor tried to enclose them in 1872,
about a thousand people marched on the land to claim it,[^bogs] peacefully: after a short march they sat down to lunch
by the river. The Corporation later bought the land for the town. A bigger play-park was built in 2003, and the park
won a Green Flag Award in 2004.
