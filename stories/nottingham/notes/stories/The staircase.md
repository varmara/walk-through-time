The Pigot family built Thrumpton Hall in rose-coloured brick, and it was largely complete by 1617. In the 1660s
Gervase Pigot altered and improved it. Its Jacobean cantilevered staircase was carved from the estate's own timber,
with John Webb, a pupil of Inigo Jones, overseeing the work.
