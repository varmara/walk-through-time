Hucknall had been a village of framework knitters. In 1861 the Hucknall Colliery Company sank its Top Pit off Watnall
Road, and in 1866 its Bottom Pit off Portland Road, and in under a century Hucknall grew into a market town. The Top
Pit closed in 1943 and the Bottom Pit in 1986.
