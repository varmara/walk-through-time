A chapel was cut into the sandstone here, about a quarter of a mile west of the castle, and from at least the late 12th
century two monks from Lenton kept it. In 1474 it passed to the King and Gervase Clifton in exchange for the chapel at
Tickhill Castle. Puritans wrecked much of it in the Civil War, calling the caves "Papist Holes", and in the mid-19th
century they were made into refreshment rooms. The site is now behind flats on Castle Boulevard.
