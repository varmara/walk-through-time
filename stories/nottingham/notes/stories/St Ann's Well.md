The district takes its name from a spring once thought to heal. It was recorded in 1301 as the Brodewell, and was
also called Robin Hood's Well; in 1500 monks built a chapel beside it and dedicated it to St Ann. A spired cover put
over it in 1856 was pulled down in 1887 for the railway, and the site was later built over by the Gardeners pub.
