Domesday Book calls the village Aitone. Its church of St Laurence stands east of the Market Place: local tradition
dates parts of it to the 11th century and King Cnut, but its oldest parts may be from after the Conquest, in the 12th
century. It was a daughter church of Sawley until the 19th century. It is listed Grade II*.
