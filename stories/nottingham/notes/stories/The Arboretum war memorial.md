The Arboretum's bell tower is a war memorial to the 59th (2nd Nottinghamshire) Regiment of Foot, designed by
Marriott Ogle Tarbotton in 1862–63. Its cupola held a bell that British troops took from a temple in Canton during
the Second Opium War, until the bell went to the regimental museum in 1956. Two of the cannon beside it were taken at
Sebastopol in the Crimean War; the other two are copies.
