In 1876–77 a viaduct was built here of wrought iron, light enough for ground undermined by coal workings. It closed
in 1968 but was too costly to take down, and in 2022 it reopened to walkers and cyclists. The only other viaduct like it
is Meldon Viaduct in Devon.
