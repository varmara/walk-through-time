Boots' central store was built on the corner of Pelham Street and High Street to designs by
[[Albert Nelson Bromley]]: 1902–05 by Historic England's dates, 1903–04 in Wikipedia's list of his works. Its
shopfront, with curving Art Nouveau panels, has hardly changed and is a rare survivor of its kind.[^he] Historic
England suggests it may be the first Art Nouveau shopfront in the country, and that Harrods inspired it.[^hc][^report]
Boots left in 1972; Zara trades here now.[^report]
