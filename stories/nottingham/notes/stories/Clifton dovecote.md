The red-brick dovecote on the green was built in the early 18th century for the Clifton family's farming
estate.[^cvra] It is said to be one of the largest in England, with about 2,300 nesting places; pigeons were kept for
winter meat.[^walk] A stone tablet on its side remembers the men of the village who died in the First World
War.[^roll] It is listed Grade II.
