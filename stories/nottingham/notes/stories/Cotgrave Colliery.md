The colliery came in the early 1960s: from 1962 hundreds of mining families moved here from Radford's closed pit,
and the village grew several times over. The pit worked through the 1984–85 strike and closed in 1993. Its pithead site is now the
Hollygate Park estate, and the spoil heap became Cotgrave Country Park.[^site] The pin is approximate.
