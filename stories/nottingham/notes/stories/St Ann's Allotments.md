In the 1830s the Clay Fields were divided into plots, and the gardens on Hunger Hill became the St Ann's Allotments,
the oldest and largest allotment site in England. Hungerhill Gardens alone has 456 plots over about 52 acres, with
sheds and summerhouses from the 19th century; the whole site is listed Grade II*.
