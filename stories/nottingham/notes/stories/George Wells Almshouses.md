Their datestone reads "George Wells, Anno 1709", though Wells's will was written in 1712. The almshouses were for six
poor unmarried or widowed women, kept up by his lands in Cropwell Bishop, Wilford and Ruddington, with Sir Gervase
Clifton and his heirs as trustees; each woman had an allowance and three tons of coal a year from Clifton
Colliery. Restored in the 1970s, they are now two homes.[^walk]
