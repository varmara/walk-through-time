A Georgian town house was built here in 1752. The Nottingham Subscription Library, founded in 1816, moved in during
1821. In 1836 a meridian line was laid so that the library's longcase clock could keep Nottingham time, 4 minutes and
33 seconds behind Greenwich, and it still does. In 1841 Alfred Barber opened the first photographic studio in the
Midlands in its attics.
