A Georgian town house built in 1752. The Nottingham Subscription Library, founded in 1816, moved in during 1821.

> [!fact]
> source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bromley_House_Library
> check: 4 minutes and 33 seconds | meridian line | 1836
>
> The longcase clock in the library is still set to Nottingham time, 4 minutes and 33 seconds behind Greenwich, by a
> meridian line laid in 1836.

> [!fact]
> source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bromley_House_Library
> check: Alfred Barber | first photographic studio in the Midlands | 1841
>
> In 1841 Alfred Barber opened the first photographic studio in the Midlands in its attics.
