E. P. Davis built the Bennerley furnaces, near Ilkeston, in about 1870; by 1880 they belonged to the Awsworth Coal
and Iron Company, which was putting up a new furnace.[^grace] A branch of the viaduct's railway served the works.
They made their last pig iron in March 1931, and in 1934 Thos. W. Ward of Sheffield bought the three furnaces to take
them down.[^grace]
