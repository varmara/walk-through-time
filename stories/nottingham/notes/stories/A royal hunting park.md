The first written record of a royal hunting lodge at Bestwood is from 1286, in the reign of Edward I. Charles II and
Nell Gwyn visited it, and in 1682 he leased the lodge to her. Their son, Charles Beauclerk, Earl of Burford, was made Duke
of St Albans in 1684, and Bestwood passed down through his descendants.
