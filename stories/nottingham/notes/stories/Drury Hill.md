A steep, narrow medieval street, at its narrowest only 4 feet 10 inches wide, that ran down from the town to Narrow
Marsh. A postern gate stood at its top when the town was walled. It was demolished in the 1960s, despite protests,
for the Broad Marsh shopping centre.
