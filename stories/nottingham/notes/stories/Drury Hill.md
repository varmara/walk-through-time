The street was first called Vault Lane, after the great rock cellars under the house where it met Low Pavement, and
later Parkyn Lane. It took its present name about 1620 from Alderman Drury, a wealthy shoemaker who lived in that
house, Vault Hall. Traffic once had to climb the cliff to the old town by Malin Hill, the Long Stairs and Hollow
Stone; it found the slope of Drury Hill, up from Narrow Marsh, much easier, and the street became the town's chief
business thoroughfare. When carts and carriages came into use in the 17th century it proved too narrow, and Hollow
Stone was eased to become a main way into the town.[^old]

At its narrowest the street was only 4 feet 10 inches wide, and a postern gate stood at its top when the town was
walled. It was demolished in the 1960s, despite protests, for the Broad Marsh shopping centre.[^lion]
