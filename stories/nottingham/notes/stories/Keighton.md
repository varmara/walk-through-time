The medieval village of Keighton stood on the hillside that is now part of University Park. It belonged to Lenton
Priory and is first mentioned in 1276; by 1387 its last five houses were in ruins. Kilns here later made roof and floor
tiles, probably for the priory. The site was found in 1940, when the field was dug for allotments, and excavated from
the 1950s.
