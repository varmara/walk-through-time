[[Godfrey Chetwynd, 8th Viscount Chetwynd|Viscount Chetwynd]] built the factory from 1915 to fill shells with
explosive; it filled some 19 million during the First World War. On 1 July 1918 eight tons of TNT exploded,
killing 134 people. The site is now Chetwynd Barracks.
