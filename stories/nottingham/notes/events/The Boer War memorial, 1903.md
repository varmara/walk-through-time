On 26 March 1903 General Lord Methuen unveiled a granite obelisk to the officers and men of the city and county of
Nottingham who died in the South African War of 1899–1902. It carries all their names and their regiments, and it
was given to the city by the lace manufacturer Thomas Isaac Birkin.[^artuk] It first stood at the bottom of King
Street, and was moved here, to the corner of the Forest, to make way for trolleybuses.[^geograph]
