On 10 October 1831, during the Reform riots, a crowd marched on Colwick Hall, the home of John Musters, and sacked
and partly burned it.[^riots] His wife, Mary Chaworth Musters, spent the night in pouring rain with her daughter Sophia,
hiding beneath the shrubbery; she died at Wiverton Hall some four months later, from the shock. The hall is now a
hotel.
