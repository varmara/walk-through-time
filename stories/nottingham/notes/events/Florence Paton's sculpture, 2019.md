On International Women's Day, 8 March 2019, a stainless steel bust of Florence Paton, Nottinghamshire's first woman
MP, was unveiled in the Garden for the Blind on Carlton Hill. She sat for Rushcliffe, which then took in Carlton,
from 1945 to 1950, and pressed for safer mines and better health care for women and children. The sculptor Hilary
Cartmel gave it miners' picks and a lamp for her ties to the area, books for her work in education, and the crowned
portcullis of Parliament.[^gedling] The garden itself, of herbs and scented plants in raised beds, was made for people
with little or no sight.[^geograph]
