In 1947 the Trent flooded 3,000 properties, 86 factories and 28 miles of road in Nottingham, and the city's first
flood defence was built that year. [Photographs of West Bridgford under water](https://inspirepicturearchive.org.uk/image/17451/The_Floods_of_1947_West_Bridgford)
are in Inspire Picture Archive. A new defence along 27 km of the river, from Sawley to Colwick,
was finished in 2012.
