A royal charter of 1284 refers to Nottingham's fairs. For centuries Goose Fair was held in the Old Market Square,
until in 1928, having outgrown the city centre, it moved to the Forest Recreation Ground. It was cancelled for the
Great Plague in 1646 and during the World Wars.
