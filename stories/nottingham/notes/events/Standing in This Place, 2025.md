On 6 February 2025 a life-size bronze by the sculptor Rachel Carter and the community history group the Legacy Makers
was unveiled in the Green Heart at Broad Marsh.[^council] It shows
two women in the dress of their time: a white woman from an East Midlands textile mill, and an enslaved Black woman
from the cotton fields of America.[^njm] It was paid for by donations of more than
£250,000, and the Art Fund helped the National Justice Museum, which looks after it, to acquire it.[^wire][^njm]
