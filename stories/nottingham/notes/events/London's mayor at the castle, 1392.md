After a riot in Fleet Street, Richard II summoned the mayor, sheriffs and aldermen of London to appear before him
and his council at Nottingham Castle on 25 June 1392. The mayor and sheriffs were removed from their offices and
imprisoned, and a royal warden was put in charge of the City: London lost its self-government until it had paid the
king to win it back.
