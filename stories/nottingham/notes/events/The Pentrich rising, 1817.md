On the night of 9–10 June 1817, men from around Pentrich in Derbyshire set out from South Wingfield to march on
Nottingham, led by Jeremiah Brandreth, the Nottingham Captain. At Giltbrook they met a small force of Light
Dragoons and scattered. The news that had set them marching was a hoax by William Oliver, a government informer;
Brandreth and two others were hanged and beheaded at Derby.
