When the House of Lords threw out the Second Reform Bill on 8 October 1831, rioting broke out in Nottingham
and a crowd burned the castle, which belonged to the [[Henry Pelham-Clinton, 4th Duke of Newcastle|Duke of Newcastle]],
an opponent of reform. Three men were hanged; the Duke refused to restore the castle, and its burnt shell stood on
the rock for years.
