In the Glorious Revolution of 1688, James II's daughter Princess Anne turned against her father. She and her friend
Sarah Churchill slipped out of Whitehall by a back staircase into the care of Bishop Compton, and reached Nottingham on
1 December. Here she and her party stayed at the Feathers Inn, lodged at the charge
of the Earl of Devonshire.[^date]
