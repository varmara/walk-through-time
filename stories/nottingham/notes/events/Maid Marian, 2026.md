On 17 July 2026 Alicja Biała's statue of Maid Marian was unveiled at the corner of Tollhouse Hill and Upper Parliament
Street. It stands on a sandstone boulder, and is made from native plants, among
them Nottingham catchfly, oak, birch, hawthorn and bluebell, cast in bronze from wax moulds.[^council] She invited more
than a hundred local people to workshops, where they brought plants, some from their own gardens; the cast pieces were welded into
one figure 2.3 m tall.[^leftlion]
