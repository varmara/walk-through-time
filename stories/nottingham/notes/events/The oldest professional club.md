Notts County, called the oldest professional football club in the world, gives 1862 as its foundation, though it was
formally organised on 7 December 1864. Its first recorded matches were at the Meadows Cricket Ground, its home until
October 1877. It played at Trent Bridge from 1883, and since 1910 at Meadow Lane, on land leased from Nottingham
Corporation. In 1894 it beat Bolton Wanderers 4–1 at Goodison Park, the first Second Division team to win the FA
Cup. Late in 1901 a set of Notts-style kits sent to Turin gave Juventus their black and white stripes.
