Bodyline, fast bowling aimed at the batsman's body, was England's tactic in the Ashes in Australia in 1932–33. Its
bowlers were Nottinghamshire's [[Harold Larwood]] and [[Bill Voce]], whom Douglas Jardine met with their county captain,
Arthur Carr, to plan it. The uproar ended Larwood's Test career and changed the laws of cricket; Trent Bridge opened the Larwood
and Voce Stand in 1985.
