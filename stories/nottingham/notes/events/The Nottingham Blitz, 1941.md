On the night of 8–9 May 1941 the Luftwaffe bombed Nottingham. Firefighters saved St Mary's Church after an
incendiary burned through its roof; three Boots factories, in Poplar Street, Island Street and Station Street, were
on fire; University College and the Shakespeare Street chapel were hit. The worst loss was at the Co-op bakery on
Meadow Lane, where 49 people died. In all, 159 people were killed that night.
