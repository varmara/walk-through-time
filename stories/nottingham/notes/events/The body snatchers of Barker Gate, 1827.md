As St Mary's churchyard filled, the parish opened burial grounds on Barker Gate, the first in 1742 on land bought
from the Duke of Kingston for ten shillings, known as Middle Bury, and another in 1786, Top Bury. In 1827
"resurrection men" tried to send two bodies to London for the anatomists; as families checked the recent graves, it
was found that thirty bodies had been stolen.[^shp] The two found were an old woman, Dorothy Townsend, and Mrs Rose's
three-year-old son. A man called Davies, thought to be one of the gang, barely escaped with his life when crowds mobbed
him in Nottingham and Arnold, and the two were buried again.[^lion]
