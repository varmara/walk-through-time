On 7 August 1844 William Saville, who had murdered his wife and their three children in Colwick Wood, was hanged in
front of the County Hall on High Pavement. A vast crowd had packed the street; the Northern Star put it at no fewer
than twenty thousand. As the drop fell the crowd surged, and many were forced down the steps from High Pavement into
Garner's Hill and trampled.[^star] Twelve were killed and more than a hundred badly hurt, five of whom died later;[^spectator][^date] one
report counted seven dead on the day.[^star] The last public hanging in Nottingham was that of Richard Thomas Parker,
on 10 August 1864.[^parker]
