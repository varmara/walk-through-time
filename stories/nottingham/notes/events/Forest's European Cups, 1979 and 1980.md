Under [[Brian Clough]], Nottingham Forest won the European Cup in Munich in May 1979, beating Malmö 1–0 with a goal
by Trevor Francis, and again in Madrid a year later, beating Hamburg 1–0 with a goal by John Robertson: back-to-back
European Cups.
