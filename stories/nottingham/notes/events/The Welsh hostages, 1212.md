King John took hostages in numbers no king had before. In 1212, when the Welsh rose against him, he came to Nottingham
with his army and, the chronicler Roger of Wendover says, before he either ate or drank had twenty-eight youths hanged,
whom he had received from the Welsh as hostages the year before.[^wendover] The Magna Carta Project puts them at some
thirty, and calls it notorious.[^mcp]
