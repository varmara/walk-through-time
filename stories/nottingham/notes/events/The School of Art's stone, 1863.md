Building on the new School of Art in Waverley Street began on 23 May 1863, but its foundation stone waited until
22 October, when the 5th Duke of Newcastle could come. He was then the Provincial Grand Master of Nottinghamshire's
Freemasons, and he laid the stone with the usual Masonic ceremonies; the band played "God Save the Queen" and the
crowd joined in.[^mmr] Thirty-five years later it was remembered as the last such ceremony the Masons had performed
in Nottingham itself.[^fcn]
