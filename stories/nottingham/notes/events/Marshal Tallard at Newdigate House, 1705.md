Newdigate House on Castle Gate, built around 1675 for Thomas Charlton the younger, of Chilwell, was where the French
commander [[Marshal Tallard]] was kept from 1705 to 1711 after his defeat at Blenheim. He lived here on parole.[^tallard] Daniel Defoe counted his small formal garden, laid out in the French fashion,
among the beauties of Nottingham, and Tallard is credited with bringing celery into English cooking.[^tallard] He
went home to France on his release in 1711.[^tallard] From 1960 the house was the United Services Club.
