For more than a century Nottingham's Freemasons had no hall of their own. One lodge, meeting in the town from 1755,
moved from inn to inn: the Red Lion on Climber Street by 1763, the Leg of Mutton on Sandy Lane, then three inns on
Parliament Street in turn, the Kingstone Arms, the New George and the Milton's Head, and later the Crown and Cushion,
the Poultry Hotel and the Castle and Falcon on Warser Gate. From 1844 it met in the Assembly Rooms on Low Pavement,
and in 1881 it settled at the Masonic Hall on Goldsmith Street.[^lane] Another lodge, named after the Milton's Head inn,
whose records run from 1790, belonged to the rival Antients; its warrant was cancelled in 1799 and restored the
next year.[^tna]
