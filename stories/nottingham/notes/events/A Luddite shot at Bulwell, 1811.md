On the night of 10 November 1811 some 20 to 30 frame-breakers walked from Arnold to Bulwell Forest, met about 40
more, and marched on the house of the hosier Edward Hollingworth, who had barricaded it and armed his friends. Shots
were exchanged in the dark; the Luddites drove the defenders out through a back window and smashed all seven frames,
with the doors, windows and furniture. At dawn the body of one of the attackers lay by the road at Bulwell Forest:
John Westley, a framework knitter who had lived in Arnold for twelve years. The inquest found the killing justifiable,
in self-defence. As far as is known, he was the only man killed in the Nottinghamshire disturbances.[^westley]
