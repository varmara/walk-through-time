In 868 a Viking army under Ivar the Boneless and Halfdan Ragnarsson took Nottingham and wintered here. Burgred of
Mercia and his West Saxon allies laid siege, but made peace and let them go in 869. From 877 the Danes settled, and
Nottingham, a town of about 39 acres, became one of the Five Boroughs of the Danelaw, until Edward of Wessex took it
in 918. King Edmund won the Five Boroughs back for the English in 942.
