On 23 August 1958 a fight in St Ann's grew into a riot between white and West Indian
residents; the police restored order within 90 minutes. A crowd of about 4,000 gathered the following weekend. With
the Notting Hill riots a week later, the disturbances led to talks between Caribbean governments and the British
government.
