William "Bendigo" Thompson, born in Nottingham on 11 October 1811, won the heavyweight championship of England in
1839. Drink took him to the house of correction 28 times, but in 1872 he heard the preacher Richard Weaver, and spent
his last years preaching himself, touring the country to large crowds. He died in 1880; thousands lined the roads for
his funeral, and he was buried in St Mary's burial ground, under a tomb with a crouching lion. The city of
Bendigo in Australia is indirectly named after him.
