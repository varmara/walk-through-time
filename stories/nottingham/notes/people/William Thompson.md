William "Bendigo" Thompson, born in Nottingham on 11 October 1811, won the heavyweight championship of England in
1839. He died in 1880 and was buried in St Mary's burial ground, under a tomb with a crouching lion. The city of
Bendigo in Australia is indirectly named after him.
