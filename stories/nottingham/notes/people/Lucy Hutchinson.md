Lucy Hutchinson, born in the Tower of London in 1620, was the first to translate the whole of Lucretius into
English verse, and her Order and Disorder is perhaps the first epic poem by a woman in English. Her life of her
husband, [[John Hutchinson]], written for her family, was printed in 1806. She died at Owthorpe in 1681.
