Harry Cursham was born in Wilford on 27 November 1859. In the FA Cup he scored 49 goals in 44 games for Notts County,
the competition's individual scoring record, and for England 5 goals in 8 games; he also played two first-class
cricket matches for Nottinghamshire. He died on 6 August 1941.
