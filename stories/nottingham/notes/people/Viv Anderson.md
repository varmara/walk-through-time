Viv Anderson was born in Clifton on 29 July 1956. In ten years at Nottingham Forest, from 1974 to 1984, he played
328 times and won the European Cup twice. In November 1978, against Czechoslovakia, he became the first black
footballer to play for England's senior men's team.
