Florence Boot, born in St Helier, Jersey, in 1863, married [[Jesse Boot]] in 1886 and helped him run the firm. She
brought books and stationery into the Goose Gate shop and started Boots Book-Lovers' Library and cafés in the
stores. She founded the university's first hall for women, named Florence Boot Hall after her.
