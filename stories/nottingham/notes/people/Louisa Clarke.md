Louisa Clarke opened a day and boarding school for girls in 1874, at 9 Park Terrace; called Western House, it moved
here, to 30 The Ropewalk, in 1890. Rose Feilmann, who wrote "There are fairies at the bottom of my garden", was one
of her pupils.
