Harold Larwood, born at Nuncargate in 1904, played for Nottinghamshire from 1924 and bowled for England in the
Bodyline series. He moved to Australia in 1950; Trent Bridge named the Larwood and Voce Stand after him and
[[Bill Voce]] in 1985.
