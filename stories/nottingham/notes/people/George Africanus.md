George John Scipio Africanus was born in Sierra Leone around 1763 and baptised in Wolverhampton in 1766 as a boy
belonging to Benjamin Molineux; he was trained as a brass founder and came to Nottingham as a young man. On 3 August
1788 he married Esther Shaw at St Peter's Church, and around 1793 the couple started Africanus' Register of Servants,
an employment agency run from their home on Chandlers Lane. On 24 October 1829 he bought the house and the
neighbouring ones in Bluchers Yard for £380. He died on 19 May 1834 and is buried in St Mary's churchyard, where a
plaque calls him Nottingham's first black entrepreneur.
