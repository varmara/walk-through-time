Jesse Boot, born in 1850, turned the business founded by his father, John Boot, into a national retailer. He was
knighted in 1909 and became Baron Trent in 1929. He gave the land at Highfields for the new University College, now
the University of Nottingham, which opened in 1928; his bust stands there. In 1920 he sold his controlling interest
to American investors. He also paid for [[The Dorothy Boot Homes, 1908|the Dorothy Boot Homes]] in Wilford.
