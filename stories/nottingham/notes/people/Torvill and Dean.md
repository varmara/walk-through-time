The ice dancers Jayne Torvill and Christopher Dean come from Nottingham. The square outside the ice centre is named
Bolero Square after their Olympic routine. Jayne Torvill was born in Clifton on 7 October 1957 and worked as an insurance clerk
at Norwich Union before skating full-time; at Sarajevo in 1984 she and Christopher Dean won the gold with their
free dance to Ravel's Boléro, which earned twelve perfect 6.0 marks.
