Mary Howitt married [[William Howitt]] in 1821, and by 1823 they were living in Nottingham, on Timber Hill, now
South Parade, next to Smith's Bank, for over twelve years.[^house] She wrote The Spider and the Fly and
translated Hans Christian Andersen's tales.
