Helen Watts, the daughter of the vicar of Lenton, joined Nottingham's suffragettes after hearing Christabel
Pankhurst speak in 1907. In 1909 she served a month in Holloway, was arrested here at the Albert Hall in July and
let go without charge, and went on hunger strike in Leicester gaol.
