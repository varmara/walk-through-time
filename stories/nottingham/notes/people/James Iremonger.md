James Iremonger was born in Norton, in the East Riding of Yorkshire, on 5 March 1876, and played for Wilford's
football club in 1893. He played for Nottingham Forest from 1895 to 1910, won two England caps in 1901–02, played
cricket for Nottinghamshire from 1899 to 1914 and coached the county from 1921 to 1938. His brother was
[[Albert Iremonger]], the Notts County goalkeeper. He died in Nottingham on 25 March 1956.
