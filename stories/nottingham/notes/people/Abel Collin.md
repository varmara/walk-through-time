Abel Collin, born in Nottingham in 1653, left money in his will of 1704 for almshouses. Those on Friar Lane, built
in 1709, were demolished in 1956 to make way for Maid Marian Way; his charity, the oldest in Nottingham, still has
houses in Beeston.
