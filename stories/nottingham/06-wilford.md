# Wilford
id: wilford

A village on the south bank of the Trent, reached for centuries by ferry and, since the 1870s, by bridge.

## Wilford Toll Bridge
- source: en:Wilford Toll Bridge
- year: 1870
- check: 1870 | ferry | 400 years | 1974

Opened in 1870, replacing a ferry that had crossed here for some 400 years. Pedestrians paid a halfpenny toll.
It was closed to traffic in 1974.

## Wilford Suspension Bridge
- source: en:Wilford Suspension Bridge
- check: Arthur Brown | Wilford Hill reservoir | water

A footbridge built by the city's water department, mainly to carry water pipes across the Trent to the
reservoir on Wilford Hill. The engineer was Arthur Brown.

## St Wilfrid's Church
- source: en:St Wilfrid's Church, Wilford
- at: name:St Wilfrid's Church
- check: late 14th century | Gervase de Wilford | 1361 | Henry Kirke White | inspiration

The church dates from the late 14th century and is thought to have been founded by Gervase de Wilford in
around 1361. Inside are two memorials to the poet Henry Kirke White, who drew much inspiration from Wilford
and Clifton.

## Ferry Inn
- source: en:Wilford
- at: name:Ferry Inn
- check: later 19th century | wilford ferry inn | many visitors from nottingham | iremonger | 1958

Wilford stayed a village until the later 19th century. With woods around it and riverside amenities such as
the Ferry Inn, it attracted many visitors from Nottingham. The Notts County goalkeeper Albert Iremonger was
its landlord until his death in 1958.

Thomas Forman, who founded the Nottingham Post, bought Wilford House from the Smith family after the
