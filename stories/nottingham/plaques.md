# Corrections to plaques

Plaques come from Nottingham City Council's list and OpenStreetMap. A row matches a plaque by the start of its
inscription or by its subject's name, and gives it a short label (for plaques to places and events), the group
a person belongs in (architects, writers, science, industry, sport, arts, faith, military, public, other), or
a type (thing, group) when it is not about a person.
