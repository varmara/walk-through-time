# Lace and trade
id: lace

In the 19th century Nottingham was a world centre of lace. Merchants built warehouses and showrooms in the
Lace Market, east of the Old Market Square.

## Adams Building
- source: en:Adams Building, Nottingham
- year: 1855
- check: Thomas Adams | Thomas Chambers Hine | 1855

A lace showroom and warehouse built for Thomas Adams to a design by Thomas Chambers Hine, opened in 1855.

## Severn's Building
- source: en:Severn's Building
- check: Castle Road

A medieval timber-framed building, moved to its present site on Castle Road.

## Victoria Centre
- source: en:Victoria Centre
- year: 1972
- check: 1972 | clock tower

A shopping centre opened in 1972 on the site of Nottingham Victoria railway station, whose clock tower still
stands beside it.

## Adams Building
- source: en:Adams Building, Nottingham
- check: Thomas Chambers Hine | Quaker | chapel | model example | 1999

Built from 1854 to designs by Thomas Chambers Hine for Thomas Adams, a Quaker lace manufacturer, with a chapel
for the workers in the basement. Inspectors thought it a model example of its kind; since 1999 it has been
part of Nottingham College.
