# Rushcliffe
id: rushcliffe

The borough south of the Trent: villages of stocking knitters, a wartime ammunition depot, and the gravel pits
that became a centre for watersports.

## Framework Knitters' Museum
- source: en:Listed buildings in Ruddington
- at: osm:way/362859588
- check: Framework Knitters | 1829 | 1840 | museum

Ruddington's framework knitters worked at hand frames in workshops behind their cottages. Workshops of about
1840 and workers' cottages of about 1829, all listed, now make up the Framework Knitters' Museum.

## Ruddington Depot
- source: en:Ruddington Depot
- at: osm:way/328346132
- check: 1940 | ammunition | 1983 | Rushcliffe Country Park

Built from 1940 to fill and store ammunition, the depot was closed in 1983. Its land is now a transport heritage
centre, a business park and Rushcliffe Country Park.

## National Watersports Centre
- source: en:Holme Pierrepont National Watersports Centre
- names: no
- check: 1971 | gravel | 1975 | 1986

Dug out of a former gravel works, the centre opened in 1971, and hosted the World Rowing Championships in 1975
and 1986. Its whitewater course was built in 1986.
