# Clifton

An old village above the Trent, and next to it one of the largest housing estates ever built by a council.

## Clifton estate
- source: en:Clifton, Nottingham
- at: pin:-1.1745,52.9045
- check: 1950 | largest council estate in europe | private ownership

Planned from 1950, the Clifton council estate was at one time the largest council estate in Europe. Since the
1980s most of its houses have passed into private ownership.

## St Mary's Church
- source: en:St Mary's Church, Clifton
- check: medieval | Cottingham | 1846 | Bodley

A medieval church, listed Grade I. Lewis Nockalls Cottingham restored it in 1846, and much of the decoration
is by George Frederick Bodley.

## Clifton Bridge
- source: en:Clifton Bridge (Nottingham)
- see: Trent Bridge | Wilford Toll Bridge
- check: 1958 | pre-stressed | Trent Bridge | 1972

Opened in 1958 to take traffic off Trent Bridge, it was then the longest pre-stressed concrete bridge in the
country. A second bridge beside it, opened in 1972, made the crossing a dual carriageway.
