# Caves
id: caves

Nottingham stands on soft sandstone that can be dug with hand tools, and about a thousand man-made caves are
known beneath it: cellars, workshops, dwellings and shelters.

## City of Caves
- source: en:City of Caves
- check: tannery | air raid shelter | only known underground tannery | 1500

Caves under the old town, used over the years as a tannery, pub cellars and an air raid shelter. Two caves
opening onto the cliff face held the only known underground tannery in Britain, set up in 1500.

## Mortimer's Hole
- source: en:Nottingham Castle
- at: pin:-1.1541,52.9496
- check: Mortimer's Hole | 1330 | secret tunnel | bound and gagged

A long passage to the bottom of the castle rock is known as Mortimer's Hole. In October 1330 Edward III's men
came in by a secret tunnel, and Roger Mortimer was led out bound and gagged.

## Sneinton Hermitage
- source: en:Sneinton
- at: pin:-1.1310,52.9489
- check: Hermitage | cave dwellings | what little remains

Cave dwellings dug into the sandstone ridge extended out into Sneinton, where they were known as the
Hermitage. What little remains can still be seen along Sneinton Hermitage.
