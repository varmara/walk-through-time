## Shire Hall
- source: en:Shire Hall, Nottingham

The county courthouse and gaol, rebuilt to a design by James Gandon. It is now the National Justice Museum.

## Old Market Square
- source: en:Old Market Square, Nottingham
- at: pin:-1.1497,52.9529
- check: largest paved squares | divided by a wall | stainless steel | 1928

One of the largest paved squares in the United Kingdom. The two towns were once divided by a wall across it,
whose line a stainless steel channel now marks; Goose Fair was held here until 1928.

## Broad Marsh
- source: en:Broad Marsh
- check: slum clearance | 25 March 1975 | partially demolished | City of Caves

After large-scale slum clearance, the Broad Marsh shopping centre was officially opened on 25 March 1975. It
closed in 2020 and was left partially demolished; caves found beneath it, dug into the soft sandstone, became
part of the City of Caves.
