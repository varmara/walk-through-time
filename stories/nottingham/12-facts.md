## Nottingham Castle
- source: en:Nottingham Castle
- check: 1831 | Reform Bill | 1878 | first municipal art gallery

Rioters burnt the Duke of Newcastle's mansion here in 1831, over his opposition to the Reform Bill. It
reopened in 1878 as the first municipal art gallery in the UK outside London.

## National Justice Museum
- source: en:National Justice Museum

front steps.
