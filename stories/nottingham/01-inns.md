# Old inns
id: inns

Three pubs in the centre each claim to be the oldest in Nottingham. A television investigation concluded that
the Salutation is the oldest building, but the Bell has been a pub the longest.

## Ye Olde Trip to Jerusalem
- source: en:Ye Olde Trip to Jerusalem
- see: City of Caves | Mortimer's Hole
- check: 1189 | sandstone

Claims to date from 1189, a date that rests on tradition rather than records. It is built against the castle
rock, with rooms cut into the sandstone.

## The Bell Inn
- source: en:The Bell Inn, Nottingham
- year: c. 1420
- check: oldest | dendrochronological | 1420 | sandstone

Claims to be the oldest pub in the city. Tree-ring dating of its timbers suggests it was built around 1420,
and its cellars are cut into the sandstone.

## Ye Olde Salutation Inn
- source: en:Ye Olde Salutation Inn
- year: c. 1360
- check: oldest | 1360 | caves

Another claimant to the title of the city's oldest pub. The core of the building dates from around 1360, and
unusually large caves lie beneath it.
